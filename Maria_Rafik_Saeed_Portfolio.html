PYTHON · SCIKIT-LEARN · NLTK · PANDAS

NLP Sentiment Analysis — Amazon Echo Reviews

Built a text-classification pipeline — custom cleaning, Bag-of-Words, Naive Bayes / Logistic Regression / Gradient Boosting — to sort thousands of Amazon Echo reviews as positive or negative.

The models hit 90% accuracy at first glance. The confusion matrix told a different story: 91% of the dataset was 5-star reviews, so the models had mostly learned to guess "positive" and were missing real negative feedback. Word clouds helped surface what actually drove sentiment — ease of use on the positive side, functional issues on the negative side.

SQL · POSTGRESQL · DATALAB

Analyzing Students' Mental Health

A study on international students in Japan found they face a higher risk of mental health difficulties, with social connectedness and acculturative stress as major predictors of depression.

I used PostgreSQL — aggregation, grouping, filtering, sorting — to dig into the raw survey data and see how the actual length of a student's stay abroad relates to those outcomes.

View on GitHub →
query.sql
SELECT length_of_stay,
  AVG(depression_score)
FROM survey_responses
GROUP BY length_of_stay
ORDER BY length_of_stay ASC;
-- longer stay → higher risk
